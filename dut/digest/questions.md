Standing questions: the reader answers each one for every run. One per line, an id, a full stop and the question.

Q1. What question did the brief or card ask, in its own words?
Q2. What was the baseline, and how was it chosen?
Q3. What was sealed before measurement, and what did it assume?
Q4. What number answers the question, against which baseline, and with what equivalence check?
Q5. Which sealed predictions held and which failed, and by how much?
Q6. What was voided, skipped or not run, and why?
Q7. What did the run cost, and how long did it take?
Q8. What would a second run change?
