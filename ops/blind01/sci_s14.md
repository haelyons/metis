It is exclusive with `args.card`, `args.units_check` and `args.units_probe`. The design is exactly `{arms, inputs, metrics, replicates, warmup, order_seed, seeds, comparisons}`:

  - `inputs`: exactly `{ids, spec}`: `ids` 1 to 10 unique non-empty strings; `spec` any JSON value (pins, cases, configuration).
  - `metrics`: 1 to 10 items, each exactly `{name, unit, what, source}`: `name` unique, matching `^[a-z][a-z0-9_]{0,30}$`; `unit` and `what` non-empty strings; `source` `"adapter"` (the arm's adapter reports it) or `"process_wall"` (the pipeline's trusted wall seconds of the invocation).

  - The interval `[lo, hi]` of the sorted statistics `s` (length `n`): for `all_pairs`, `[s[0], s[n-1]]`, requiring `n >= 2`; for `sign_test`, `[s[k-1], s[n-k]]` with the largest `k >= 1` such that `P(Binomial(n, 0.5) < k) <= (1 - confidence) / 2`.
