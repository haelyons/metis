# Sealed operator predictions: shakedown-m5 runs 17 to 19 (sealed comparison isolation probe)

Written and committed before launch, after the U57/U58 fix round that followed the Codex review (contract 77ebbcc5). Public, non-blind. The harness is operator-written and hostile on purpose: `cmp17/`, `cmp18/` and `cmp19/` each hold `design.json` and `harness/` (adapters `arms/A0.sh` = `arms/A1.sh`, `evaluate.sh`, `fetch_private.sh`, `versions.sh`, `cmp_files.json`, `cmp_extract.json`; `setup.sh`, `run_anchors.sh` and `run_command.sh` from run11). The arm and evaluator parts try what the contract forbids and record each attempt in `out/probe.json` and `eval/probe.json`; the sealed metrics `arm_leaks`, `eval_leaks`, `arm_controls_failed` and `eval_controls_failed` count them. The design carries canary numbers (K2 to K5 margins, threshold and reference) that the probes search for on the box. Probabilities are mine; intervals are 80%.
