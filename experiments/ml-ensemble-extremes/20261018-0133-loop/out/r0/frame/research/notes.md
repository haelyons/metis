# Working notes — programmes that would use the ml-ensemble-extremes result

Card: ensemble size (10–1,000) for rare extremes in AIFS-ENS v1 (afCRPS-trained, exchangeable members). Root question: do more members buy better extreme forecasts, or does shared model error cap skill?

## Candidates considered
- **ECMWF AIFS-ENS** — the direct target (operational AI ensemble; the card literally runs AIFS-ENS v1). PICK.
- **Anemoi (ECMWF open-source framework)** — the codebase AIFS is built on (anemoi-inference generates members). PICK.
- **NVIDIA Earth-2 / HENS (Huge Ensembles)** — closest prior (7,424 SFNO members, [s_hens]); already did huge-ensemble-for-extremes but on SFNO-BVMC, not an afCRPS single-distribution model. PICK.
- Rejected: Google DeepMind GenCast / FGN (different probabilistic approach; not the afCRPS exchangeable-member target), ai-models (ECMWF-lab archived runner — explicitly "Archived"/not maintained), FourCastNet alone (superseded by HENS/Earth-2 framing).

## Key evidence (verbatim)

### ECMWF AIFS-ENS
- "AIFS ENS becomes operational" (Newsletter 185, Autumn 2025): https://www.ecmwf.int/en/newsletter/185/earth-system-science/aifs-ens-becomes-operational
  - "AIFS ENS currently has 50 perturbed members and one control member."
  - "When generating forecasts, as many members as required can be generated by providing different random seeds to the model."
  - "Two ensemble members are used in training."
  - Authors: Simon Lang, Linus Magnusson.
- AIFS-CRPS paper (npj AI, 2026): https://www.nature.com/articles/s44387-026-00073-7
  - "The trained model is stochastic and can generate as many exchangeable members as desired."
  - "a single member 15 day forecast is created in about one minute for AIFS-CRPS O96 and four minutes for AIFS-CRPS N320 on an NVIDIA A100 40 GB GPU, including the time spent reading the initial state and writing the forecast to disk."  ← card's "4-minute N320 member time" check-first figure.
  - "An important next step is the assessment of the forecast skill of extreme events. Here, CRPS can have limited sensitivity to tail properties of distributions."  ← extremes flagged as NOT yet done.
  - "AIFS-ENS, ECMWF's now-operational real-time AIFS ensemble forecasting system, is based on AIFS-CRPS N320 described here."
- AIFS Machine Learning data page (activity, v2 upgrade): https://www.ecmwf.int/en/forecasts/datasets/aifs-machine-learning-data
  - "The ensemble model has been running operationally since 1 July 2025 and was upgraded from AIFS ENS v1 to AIFS ENS v2 on 12 May 2026."

### Anemoi
- README: https://github.com/ecmwf/anemoi
  - "Anemoi is an open-source, Python-based framework developed collaboratively by ECMWF and several European national meteorological services."
  - "anemoi-inference: Tools for deploying trained models and performing inference."
- PyPI anemoi-inference 0.12.0: https://pypi.org/project/anemoi-inference/0.12.0/
  - "Released: Sep 7, 2026" — "Latest release"; "This software is Incubating and subject to ECMWF's guidelines on Software Maturity."

### NVIDIA Earth-2 / HENS
- GMD Part 2 (4 Sep 2025): https://gmd.copernicus.org/articles/18/5605/2025/gmd-18-5605-2025.html
  - "Here in Part 2, we generate a huge ensemble (HENS), with 7424 members initialized each day of summer 2023."
  - "we combine these two perturbation techniques to create a 7424-member ensemble: 256 initial condition perturbations for each of the 29 SFNO checkpoints."
  - "The 7424-member ensemble has an information gain of 4." (and abstract: "HENS samples events 4σ away from the ensemble mean.")
  - "If the tails of the forecast distribution were Gaussian, sampling 4 standard deviation events from the ensemble mean would require approximately 7000 members."
  - "Generating the ensemble for each initial date requires 21 TB of space and 45 min using 256 80 GB NVIDIA A100 GPUs."
- NVIDIA developer blog (19 Sep 2025): https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/predict-extreme-weather-events-in-minutes-without-a-supercomputer
  - "Due to this expense, traditional weather models can only have 50 ensemble members."
  - "It is able to catch 96% of rare but severe extreme weather events that other models usually miss."
- NVIDIA Earth-2 open models launch (26 Jan 2026, activity): https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-earth-2-open-models/
  - "NVIDIA today unveiled a new NVIDIA Earth-2 family of open models, libraries and frameworks for weather and climate AI, offering the world's first fully open, accelerated weather AI software stack."

## Gaps / caveats
- 38 GB memory figure (card check-first) not located on a public page; only the 4-min/member figure was confirmed verbatim.
- No public ECMWF statement found that they have internally tested AIFS-ENS >50 members for extremes; the paper only flags it as a next step.
- NVIDIA HENS used SFNO-BVMC (29 independent checkpoints + bred vectors), a *multi-checkpoint* ensemble, so its huge-ensemble result does not directly answer whether a *single* afCRPS-trained distribution (AIFS-ENS) keeps gaining past 50 members.
