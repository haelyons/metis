# Working notes — programmes fed by card V3 (computed capsid–receptor binding calls)

Card: V3 "Motif calls emitted for Capsida NR001–NR005 and Lir Starfish binding-assay readouts".
Root question: which AAV variants transcytose the BBB vs stay intracellular, for which capsid–receptor binding signatures.
Card's step: computed bind/no-bind calls (docking + AlphaFold-Multimer seeds + MD) for 36 capsid–receptor pairs (ALPL, AAVR, LRP6 panel), scored on published labels with threefold-axis interface geometry; compute lever 54→432 runs.
Predicted outcomes: if_lever = balanced accuracy 0.80 at 432 runs vs 0.55 at 54, 5/6 binders within 5 Å of published sites; if_not = accuracy moves <0.05, ≤3/6 binders reach sites.

## Programme picks (1–3)

1. **Capsida Biotherapeutics** — the option explicitly frames the output format as their NR001–NR005 plate- and bead-based receptor binding assays. Consumer of computed calls as a pre-filter for their wet-lab binding step.
2. **Lir Therapeutics (Starfish™ / nAAVigator®)** — the option explicitly names "Lir's Starfish capsid-structure pipeline"; their docking-at-symmetry-axes step is exactly what the card scores.
3. **CLOVER Center / Gradinaru Laboratory, Caltech (APPRAISE)** — the closest prior programme (Shay et al. 2024 is the card's closest_prior) and the publisher of APPRAISE, the open-source binding-propensity tool. Note: Gradinaru is co-founder/BoD member of Capsida (CaltechAUTHORS conflict statement), so picks 1 and 3 are linked.

## Effect of the two outcomes (draft)
- Capsida if_lever: in silico bind/no-bind gate ahead of plate/bead assays; threefold-site recovery becomes interface QC; hundreds of runs budgeted as validation for patented receptor panel. if_not: no change — wet-lab binding detection remains the gate; computed calls stay hypothesis-generating.
- Lir if_lever: docking promoted from ranking heuristic to validated bind/no-bind call at the threefold patch; hundreds of pose/seed/MD runs budgeted in nAAVigator; no-bind pairs dropped before lab-in-the-loop. if_not: docking stays a ranking signal ("additive" with language models); surface-size tuning remains the only knob; extra pose sampling not justified.
- CLOVER if_lever: APPRAISE/AF-Multimer scaled from 5–10 models per competition to hundreds of runs per pair; balanced-accuracy gating before SPR/cryo-ET; threefold-site tolerance becomes standard interface criterion. if_not: models stay illustrative; calls continue to come from SPR/pull-down/cryo-ET; APPRAISE remains rank-order hints with known false negatives.

## Gaps
- Public labels cover 13/36 pairs (card); Capsida NR001–NR005 receptor identities and assay labels are proprietary/patented — full grid can't be scored on Capsida's own labels.
- No programme has published a 54→432 run-count scaling curve for binding-call accuracy; the lever question is open for all three.
- Lir's docking benchmarks are blog/poster material (ESGCT 2025), not a peer-reviewed accuracy dataset; call accuracy not externally checkable.
- Card yields binding-call agreement and interface geometry only; all three decide ultimately on transduction/transcytosis/in vivo data.
- Capsida's current priority receptors (ADAM15, RECK) are outside the card's reference labels and public interface structures.
